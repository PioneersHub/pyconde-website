{# Latest Videos ───────────── Surfaces the newest conference recordings on the front page. Walks current /talks + every /archive/{year}/talks, keeps talks with a youtube_id (not do_not_record), orders by edition year descending and then by video_published_at within an edition, renders `count` cards, and emits an ItemList JSON-LD. Ordering is by edition rather than upload date because several editions have recordings with no upload date at all — see the note in the macro body. Both video sections use it, so they slide identically — same component, same controls, same keyboard and swipe handling as the masterclasses carousel (assets/static/js/carousel.js). Those would sort to the bottom behind older but dated editions, so the freshest edition would be missing from a shelf titled "freshly released".
